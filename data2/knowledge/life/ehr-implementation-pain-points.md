# ehr-implementation-pain-points

**Pain Points and Addressing Challenges in Implementing Electronic Health Records (EHRs)**
======================================================

### Communication with Users

When implementing EHRs, one of the specific pain points is communication with users. In healthcare settings, there are various types of users, including clinicians, nurses, administrative personnel, each with their own priorities and agendas to fulfill. To successfully implement an EHR system, it's essential to get the requirements right so that it caters to everyone's needs.
