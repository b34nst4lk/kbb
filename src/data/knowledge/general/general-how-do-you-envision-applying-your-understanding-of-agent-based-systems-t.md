# How to Apply Agent-Based Systems to Real-World Problems
## Overview

Agent-based systems are useful for dealing with problems that require delegation, trust, and autonomy. To apply agent-based systems effectively, consider the following:

### Analytical Approach

1. **Assess Problem Complexity**: Determine if the problem requires an agent-based approach.
2. **Identify Team Members**: Imagine who would be part of your team to solve the problem.
3. **Chaining Work Transitions**: Think about how work can be handed off from one agent to another.
4. **Review and Accountability**: Consider how you would review and take accountability for the final results.

## Real-World Example: Email Prioritization

A possible application of agent-based systems is prioritizing emails based on certain parameters. A small group of agents could be employed to:
1. Focus on different types of emails (e.g., work-related emails go to an agent with context about the individual's work).
2. Decide whether an email can be ignored or deferred based on its content.
3. Review these against a todo list that the individual may already have planned.
4. Propose prioritization based on known context.
